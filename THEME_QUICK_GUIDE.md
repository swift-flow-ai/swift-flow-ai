# Quick Guide: Changing Theme

## Step 1: Navigate to Settings
Click on **"Settings"** in the sidebar navigation

## Step 2: Find Appearance Section
Scroll to the **"Appearance"** section (second card from the top)

## Step 3: Choose Your Theme
Click on one of three theme options:

### 🌞 Light
- Bright, clean interface
- Best for well-lit environments

### 🌙 Dark
- Easy on the eyes
- Best for low-light conditions
- **Currently default**

### 💻 System
- Follows your OS theme
- Automatically switches with your system
